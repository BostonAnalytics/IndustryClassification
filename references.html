References and source provenance

Citation system

The project uses one bibliography, reference.bib, for the website and Word report. Citations link to formatted entries on each page. The catalog below includes all records; research claims cite the relevant source at the point of use. The singular filename follows the user’s request; Module 1 suggests the equivalent name references.bib (AD688 course materials, n.d.-a).

Research source boundaries

The reconstruction uses Goindani et al. (2017), with its methods and numerical tables checked against the supplied PDF. The 2018 article by Chern et al. (2018) is separate related work; its random-forest results must not be attributed to the original SVM/GBDT paper. The five-source review appears in the research introduction.

The Census pages establish the historical sector labels (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.-a, n.d.-b). The 2022 manual anchors the current scope (Executive Office of the President, Office of Management and Budget 2022). Neither provides evidence of current career prospects.

Dataset and course provenance

The local dataset is cited as a snapshot with its input hashes retained in data/study/run.json (Jobs_2026_US 2026). Its publisher and full collection coverage have not been independently established. No public dataset DOI is invented.

The user supplied guides from D:/Repositories/AD688-Web-Analytics: M1/M01_Proj.qmd, M3/M03_Proj.qmd, M4/M04_Proj.qmd and M5/M05_Proj.qmd (AD688 course materials, n.d.-a, n.d.-b, n.d.-c, n.d.-d). The M03 file substitutes for the unavailable requested M3/M02_Proj.qmd. These are local provenance paths, not public links.

Verification limits

Publisher records were inspected for the 2018 article, OECD report and scikit-learn paper; the official Census manual was checked for sector 62. The 2017 DOI and conference metadata were found in the author-uploaded publication record. Its DOI endpoint could not be opened by the web tool, so live DOI resolution remains unverified. No missing page range was guessed. The repository’s docs/citation-audit.md records these checks.
