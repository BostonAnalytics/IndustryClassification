What the counts mean

  • Posting records: rows in the full US snapshot, with duplicate advertisements retained. These are not independently deduplicated vacancies or a count of currently live jobs.
  • Distinct employer names: non-placeholder company names, normalized for capitalization and whitespace. They are not verified legal entities. One employer can appear in several industries, so employer counts must not be added across rows.
  • Model training sample: no training sample is used in this explorer. It describes existing labels across the complete snapshot and does not report predictive accuracy.
  • Coverage: populated codes of the expected numeric length. Counts overlap across NAICS levels. A populated classification is not independent verification of an employer or worksite industry.

Industry codes and titles are stored separately. The website displays the source NAICS 2022 title; conflicting title variants, if present, remain counted in the download’s title_variants column.
