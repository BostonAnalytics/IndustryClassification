Candidate matches are not verified employer links. They use exact normalized filed names and filings dated no later than the posting. No confirmed employer CIK or job classification is inferred from a name match. These preview results have not been written into the published job snapshots.

Understand the evidence

Posting records count source rows, including repeated advertisements. Employer counts count distinct normalized company names and are not a count of jobs. Employers can appear in several industries. Training samples belong to a separate modeling experiment; this explorer does not train or evaluate a classifier.

The Data Exploration page explains coverage and verification. The Modeling page defines how a future classifier should be evaluated separately from existing source labels.

The website publishes aggregates only. Raw source data remains in the dataset folders.
